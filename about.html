Who We Are

Practical experience in California land use, permitting, and project review — focused on helping people understand what they’re getting into before they commit time, money, and resources.

Our Approach

We’ve seen firsthand how overwhelming the permitting and development process can feel — especially for homeowners and small property owners who don’t deal with this every day. Between unfamiliar terminology, unclear requirements, and long timelines, it’s easy to feel like you need to hire someone just to figure out where to start.

In many cases, that uncertainty leads people to bring on full-service project managers or consultants earlier than necessary, often at significant cost. While those services absolutely have their place, they are not always needed at the very beginning.

Our goal is to give you a clear starting point. We help you understand what you’re likely dealing with, what questions you should be asking, and what issues may come up — all in plain, straightforward language.

We believe that with the right information, many people are more capable than they think. This service is built to reduce the learning curve, remove the jargon barrier, and give you the confidence to move forward on your own terms — whether that means handling things yourself or bringing in help later, when you actually need it.

Experience

Our team brings experience working across multiple California jurisdictions, each with its own codes, processes, and expectations. This includes direct exposure to how zoning standards, development regulations, and permitting workflows are applied in practice — not just how they are written.

That multi-jurisdiction perspective provides a broader understanding of how different agencies approach project review, what tends to raise questions, and where applicants commonly run into delays.

We also have experience with projects subject to environmental review, including work associated with CEQA documentation, initial studies, and early-stage environmental considerations. This informs a practical understanding of how environmental factors can influence project feasibility, scope, and timelines.

Across both residential and commercial contexts, we have supported a range of project types — from small home-based uses and property improvements to tenant improvements and use changes. This experience provides insight into how projects are evaluated, what common issues arise, and how to better prepare before engaging with a jurisdiction.

Types of Projects We Help With

If you’re trying to figure out “can I do this?” or “what am I getting into?” — this is exactly where we help.

Home Projects

Thinking about converting a garage, adding a small unit, or making changes to your property? We help you understand what might be allowed, what could be an issue, and what to expect before you start.

Starting or Running a Business

Whether it’s a home-based business or a new commercial space, we help you understand what’s typically required, what could trigger additional review, and how to avoid surprises.

Existing Project Issues

Got comments back that don’t make sense? We break them down into plain English and help you understand what’s actually being asked.

Before You Hire a Consultant

Not sure if you need an architect, engineer, or permit expediter yet? We help you understand the scope of your project so you can make that decision with confidence.

“Can I Do This?” Questions

Sometimes you just need a quick, honest read on an idea. We help you sanity-check your project before you invest time and money going down the wrong path.

Early Project Planning
